What does the free extension do?

It captures a full web page, the visible area, or a selected region, then lets you crop, annotate, copy, and save PNG or JPG images on your device. Capture, crop, and annotate are free and unlimited. Screenshots are not uploaded.

Why can’t I capture some pages?

Chrome blocks extensions on browser pages such as chrome://, Settings, the New Tab page, and the Chrome Web Store. Local files need Chrome’s “Allow access to file URLs” setting if you want to capture them.

A very long page was cut off

There is a maximum capture height (default 16,384 CSS pixels, up to 32,766). Open the editor and use “Capture more”, or raise the limit in the extension popup, then continue.

Where are my screenshots stored?

Only on your device. Unsaved captures stay in the browser for one hour, then they are deleted. Use Save PNG, Save JPG, or Copy if you want to keep them.

What is Pro?

Snap Full Page Pro is $9.99/year, sold by Polar (merchant of record). A license key unlocks PDF export and auto-blur of emails, phone numbers, and card numbers on up to 3 devices. Capture, crop, and annotate stay free. Enter the key in the extension popup or the Pro dialog. Watermark templates and all-tabs capture are not in version 0.3.0.

When a license key is active, the extension contacts only https://api.polar.sh and sends only the license key (with Polar’s organization id and activation id) to activate, validate, or deactivate it. If Polar cannot be reached, Pro remains unlocked for up to 7 days after the last successful check.

Refunds and cancellation

Polar processes the charge. Open https://polar.sh/doosi/portal to manage or cancel the subscription and to request a refund. The receipt email from Polar links to the same portal. For help with the extension itself, email kimdoosi@kimdoosi.com. Google and the Chrome Web Store do not process this purchase.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Screenshots, page URLs, and annotations never leave your device; only a Pro license key (if you enter one) is sent to Polar.

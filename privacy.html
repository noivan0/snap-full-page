Privacy Policy

Snap Full Page (Chrome extension)
Last updated: 16 November 2026 (Asia/Seoul)
Version described: 0.2.0
Contact: kimdoosi@kimdoosi.com

This policy describes how the Snap Full Page browser extension (“the extension”) handles information. It applies to the Chrome Web Store listing and to the installed extension.

We do not collect user data

The extension does not collect, sell, share, or transmit your screenshots, browsing history, or any other user data to us or to any third party. It does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social-media SDKs.

When you take a screenshot, pixels are captured in the current tab and processed only on your device. Saved files stay wherever you choose to store them (for example, your Downloads folder or clipboard).

What stays on your device

  • Screenshot images, including crop and annotation data, are stored locally in the browser (IndexedDB) so you can edit them.
  • Local captures are automatically removed after one hour, or when you clear the extension’s site data.
  • Optional settings (for example, maximum capture height, or a local developer flag used only during testing) are stored in the same local database.
  • No account is created with us to use the free features.

Permissions

The published extension requests only activeTab and scripting. These are used when you click the toolbar icon or a capture command, so the extension can take and stitch a screenshot of the tab you are looking at. The extension does not run on every website in the background, and it does not read pages you have not asked it to capture.

Pro features and payments (not available in 0.2.0)

PDF export and sensitive-information blur are included in the 0.2.0 editor but are locked unless a local test flag is turned on. They are not for sale in this version. Payment is not integrated. There is no in-app purchase and no payment processor in the extension.

If a paid Pro plan is offered later, purchases would be handled by a third-party payments provider (not Google). Before anything is sold, this policy, the Chrome Web Store listing, and the support page will be updated to name the provider, describe what license or account data is exchanged, and explain how to request a refund or cancellation. Until then, the extension still does not send any data off your device for licensing.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, when a change is material, the Chrome Web Store listing or an in-extension notice.

Contact

Questions about this policy: kimdoosi@kimdoosi.com.

스크린샷은 현재 탭에서 촬영되며 사용자 기기 안에서만 처리됩니다. 편집용 이미지는 브라우저의 IndexedDB에 잠시 저장되고 1시간 후 삭제됩니다. 게시 버전은 activeTab과 scripting 권한만 사용하며, 도구 모음 아이콘이나 캡처 명령을 클릭했을 때만 해당 탭에 접근합니다.

0.2.0의 PDF 보내기와 민감정보 흐리기는 구현되어 있으나 결제 연동이 없어 판매되지 않습니다. 유료 Pro가 나중에 제공될 경우, Google이 아닌 제3자 결제 업체를 사용하며, 판매 전에 이 방침과 스토어 등록 정보, 지원 페이지를 업데이트합니다.
